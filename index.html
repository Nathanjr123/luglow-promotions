About us

We work at the intersection of creativity, marketing and consumer engagement, helping brands communicate their identity, reach new audiences and build lasting connections.

Who we are

Luglow Promotions is a South African marketing agency providing integrated marketing, promotional and brand development solutions to businesses, established brands and organisations.

Our services cover influencer management, digital marketing, social media management, brand activations, experiential marketing, promotional staffing and event marketing.

With experience across beverage brands, nightlife promotions, retail activations and influencer collaborations, we understand the importance of strategic thinking, professional execution and effective brand representation.
